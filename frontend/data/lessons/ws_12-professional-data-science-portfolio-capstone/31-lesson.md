# Lesson 31: Capstone: Final Report and Presentation

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to Write and present the final capstone report with problem, data provenance, preparation, methods, validation, results, limitations, ethics and recommendations; and answer reviewer questions.

## 1. Core explanation
The final report connects the problem, data, method, evidence and recommendation. Include an executive summary, question, data source and licence, preparation, analysis, results, limitations and next steps. Charts need clear labels, units, source notes and appropriate scales.

The presentation is shorter than the report and focuses on the decision. Show the strongest evidence, explain what it means and state what remains uncertain. Keep technical detail available for questions or an appendix. Rehearse and test visuals at presentation size.

Do not overstate results. Identify the population and period covered. Explain whether the work is descriptive, predictive or causal. Recommendations must be proportional to evidence and include what further work would change the decision.

## 3. Capstone application
Maintain a decision log and connect every analysis step to the project charter. Preserve provenance and reproducibility. Separate observed results from interpretation and recommendations.

## 4. Guided practice

## 6. Knowledge check

2. Which assumptions or limitations affect the conclusion?
